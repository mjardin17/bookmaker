# AI Video & Free Image Studio Documentation
**Empire OS Publishing Division**

## 1. Overview of Open Source & Free Video/Image Engines
Based on our research across GitHub repositories (including `Horde-AI/haapi`, `pollinations/pollinations`, `Wan-Video/Wan2.2`, `THUDM/CogVideo`, `Lightricks/LTX-2`, `hpcaitech/Open-Sora`, and `lcy362/agnes-video-generator`), we have incorporated multiple distinct ways to generate video and free AI images into Empire OS:

### Multiple Video Creation Methods:
1. **Google Veo 3.1 Fast (`veo-3.1-fast-generate-preview`)**:
   - Neural temporal generative video model.
   - High-fidelity optical flow vectors and character consistency.
   - Outputs 720p MP4 in 16:9 Landscape or 9:16 Vertical format.

2. **Wan 2.1 Open Weights Router (`wan`)**:
   - SOTA open weights text-to-video / image-to-video architecture based on the latest 2026 Alibaba Wan-Video open weights.
   - Coupled with directional camera motion vectors (dolly, pan, orbit, tilt).

3. **CogVideoX 1.5 Diffusion (`cogvideo`)**:
   - Open source 3D VAE + spatial-temporal transformer architecture from THUDM.
   - Excels at dynamic camera motion and particle physics.

4. **HyperFrames 2.5D Kinetic Parallax Engine (`hyperframes`)**:
   - 100% Free, unlimited, zero-credit local rendering pipeline.
   - Uses FFmpeg multi-filter kinematics (`zoompan`, dynamic contrast/brightness modulation, particle flares).
   - Generates instant 60fps MP4 video with no API quota or external service dependency.

5. **Multi-Scene Storyboard Trailer Reel Maker (`story-reel`)**:
   - Sequences 2 to 5 book chapter illustrations into an animated promotional teaser trailer.
   - Applies alternating camera trajectories (zoom-in, tracking pan, panoramic sweep, spark pulse) with automated transitions.
   - Integrates procedural high-fidelity orchestral audio soundtrack generated by the Empire OS Sound Lab Engine.

## 3. API Endpoints
- `POST /api/generate-video`:
  - Body: `{ imageBase64, imagePath, prompt, aspectRatio, engine, motionType, mode, duration, includeAudio, audioGenre, scenes }`
- `POST /api/generate-image-free`:
  - Body: `{ prompt, width, height, model, style, aspectRatio, seed }`
- `POST /api/create-story-reel`:
  - Body: `{ title, scenes: [{ imagePath, caption }], aspectRatio, audioGenre }`
- `POST /api/video-status`:
  - Body: `{ operationId, operationName }`
- `GET /api/video-file/:id`:
  - Streams rendered MP4 video file.
